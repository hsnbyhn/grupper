Kotlin 2.0 is finally here! What are your favorite new features?

I've been playing with the new K2 compiler for the past few weeks and I have to say - the performance improvements are incredible! Compilation times have dropped by almost 50% in our large project.

• K2 compiler with much faster compilation
• Stable context receivers
• New enum entries
• Better smart casts

What are your thoughts? What features are you most excited about?
